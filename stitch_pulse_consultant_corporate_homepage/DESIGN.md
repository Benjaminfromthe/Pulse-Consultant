## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional aesthetic crafted for elite financial advisory, strategic consulting, and corporate governance. Drawing inspiration from modern high-end editorial publications and heritage financial institutions, it pairs classical prestige with contemporary executive clarity. 

The aesthetic is built on high typographic contrast, architectural discipline, and deliberate refinement. The emotional response evoked should be unwavering stability, intellectual rigor, discretionary luxury, and meticulous attention to detail. 

The visual movement unites **Modern Executive Minimal** with **Editorial Prestige**:
- Crisp, hairline architectural borders with subtle warm metallic accents.
- Deep, anchor-weight navy structures contrasting against pristine porcelain surfaces.
- High-contrast serif headlines delivering poise and gravitas, supported by a precision-engineered geometric sans for dense analytics and data clarity.
- Restrained visual embellishments where micro-interactions and typographic balance replace decorative clutter.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep institutional hierarchy designed to project security, legacy, and performance. 

- **Primary Accent (`#087F75` - Deep Mineral Teal):** Serves as the operational pulse of the interface. Reserved for high-value interactive states, decisive primary actions, confirmed progress indicators, and validated statuses.
- **Institutional Core / Secondary (`#10243D` - Deep Navy):** The structural backbone. Applied across navigation rails, executive headers, primary footer anchors, card titles, and high-impact framing elements.
- **Prestige Accent / Tertiary (`#C5A15B` - Heritage Gold):** Deployed with strict restraint for advisory status badging, VIP metric indicators, bespoke highlights, active hairline rules, and strategic advisory notes.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** Anchors high-contrast typographic hierarchy for maximum legibility in complex reports and tabular records.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#475569` - Slate 600):** Contextual guidance, metadata, supporting copy, and dormant interactive borders.
- **Canvas (`#F5F7FA` - Off-White):** A soft, low-fatigue archival background providing separation from bright white interactive cards.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Elevation platform for data containers, dossiers, modals, and analytical dashboards.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Architectural boundary lines, selectively illuminated by hairline accents of gold (`#C5A15B`) to mark active context or premium tiering.

## Typography

The typographical pairing expresses an intentional dialogue between classic editorial stature and contemporary technical execution.

- **Headlines & Executive Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Employed for strategic statements, executive summaries, page headings, and primary quantitative milestones. The transitional serif letterforms express heritage and intellectual weight. It should always appear in balanced title-case or sentence-case, never in aggressive full uppercase.
- **Analytical & Functional Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for high legibility, clean horizontal geometry, and rendering fidelity in data tables, portfolio breakdowns, and operational UI components.
- **Labels & Micro-Metrics (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Medium to bold weights paired with positive letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) are strictly reserved for metric labels, transaction categories, status chips, table headers, and navigational meta-tags.
- **Tabular Figures:** When presenting financial ledgers, audit trails, and multi-currency values, font configurations must leverage tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment of numerical assets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an architectural 12-column grid system calibrated for executive dashboards, dossier documentation, and high-density financial tables.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max canvas constraint of `1440px`, with `margin-desktop` of `3rem` and `gutter-desktop` of `2rem`. Supports asymmetric layouts (e.g., 4-column contextual navigation / executive summary pinned beside an 8-column analytical data stream).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin` of `1.5rem` and `gutter` of `1.5rem`. Secondary panels fold into structured tab navigation or drawer views.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. Touch targets conform to a minimum interactive height of `48px` to preserve ergonomics while maintaining formal aesthetics.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Built strictly on multiples of `0.25rem` (4px base unit). Section intervals within executive summaries utilize `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` to preserve editorial breathing room. Form controls, metrics, and list groups deploy tight cadence (`space-xs` through `space-md`) to ensure unified data grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through crisp structural delineations and layered ambient depth rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Flat Architectural Baseline:** The base layer relies on structural borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) over `#FFFFFF` panels set against the neutral `#F5F7FA` foundation canvas.
- **Subtle Layered Ambient Shadows:**
  - *Level 1 (Interactive Cards & Ledger Rows):* `0px 1px 3px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.04), 0px 4px 8px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.02)`.
  - *Level 2 (Executive Panels & Dropdowns):* `0px 4px 12px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.06), 0px 1px 2px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.04)`.
  - *Level 3 (Modals & Strategic Overlays):* `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.06)`.
- **Gold Accent Highlighting:** Rather than heavy shadows to denote emphasis or focus, prominent panels and active audit lines utilize a directional border-top or border-left highlight (`2px solid #C5A15B`).
- **Dark Inversion Tier:** Deep institutional sections (Header navbars, executive terminal sidebars, and critical action footers) employ `#10243D` with subtle hairline interior borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), imparting an elite, sovereign finish.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, soft architectural radius (`roundedness: 1`), reinforcing precision, institutional solidity, and timeless executive taste. Curvature is kept restrained to avoid juvenile or casual expressions.

- **Default Form & Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Standard radius of `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`), delivering clean corners with just enough softening to prevent visual harshness.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem`, providing structured containment for data blocks, metric widgets, and document cards.
- **Large Modals and Executive Drawers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem`, applied strictly to master dialogs and high-level analytical sheets.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Pure pill radii (`9999px`) are restricted entirely to micro status badges, advisory tags, and analytical filter chips to establish distinct functional separation from structural cards and primary action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Teal):** Background `#087F75`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid transparent`, radius `0.25rem`. On hover: `#066860`. Focus: Ring of `2px #087F75` offset by `2px #FFFFFF`.
- **Secondary Institutional (Navy):** Background `#10243D`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid transparent`. On hover: `#1B3659`.
- **Tertiary / Advisory Outline:** Background `transparent`, text `#10243D`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. On hover: Border `#C5A15B`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#087F75`.
- **Mobile Touch Height:** All button variants enforce a minimum height of `48px` on mobile screens with horizontal padding of `space-lg` (`1.5rem`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicator Badges:** Full pill radius (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`. Typography `label-sm`.
- **Advisory / VIP Tier:** Soft gold background `rgba(197, 161, 91, 0.12)`, text `#876828`, border `1px solid rgba(197, 161, 91, 0.3)`.
- **Operational / Verified:** Teal tint background `rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.08)`, text `#087F75`, border `1px solid rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.25)`.
- **Neutral / Meta:** Background `#F5F7FA`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.25rem`, height `44px` (desktop) and `48px` (mobile), padding `0 1rem`. Typography `body-md` in `#0F172A`. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- **Active / Focused:** Border transitions smoothly to `1px solid #087F75` with a subtle ring of `3px rgba(8, 127, 117, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Square (`0.25rem` radius) or circle, `18px` dimension, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state features background `#087F75` with a crisp `#FFFFFF` icon.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `space-xl` (`2rem`).
- **Executive Metric Card:** Features a `2px solid #C5A15B` top hairline border. Numerical stat rendered in `Playfair Display` (`headline-lg`), with the sub-descriptor rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`label-md`, `#475569`, uppercase).

### Lists & Financial Tables
- **Table Headers:** Background `#F5F7FA`, border-bottom `1px solid #E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm` uppercase in `#475569`.
- **Data Rows:** Zebra-striping avoided in favor of clean hairline horizontal borders (`1px solid #F1F5F9`). Row hover effect introduces subtle illumination (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Ledger Alignment:** Text aligns left, metrics and financial valuations align strictly right using tabular numerics.

### Executive Alert Banners
- **Institutional Advisory Callout:** Left-side accented bar (`3px solid #C5A15B`), background `#FAF9F5`, text `#10243D`, paired with concise metadata rendered in `#087F75`.
